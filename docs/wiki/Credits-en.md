# Credits

Bioluma is **free**, has **no ads** and **uses no AI-generated art**. This is everything it owes to other people. Thank you!

## Bert Chan and Lenia

The creatures, their Latin names and their numbers come from **Lenia**, created by **Bert Wang-Chak Chan**. Bioluma uses 26 species from his catalog, with their parameters unchanged.

- Project and catalog: [github.com/Chakazul/Lenia](https://github.com/Chakazul/Lenia)
- Catalog license: *MIT License, Copyright (c) 2018 Bert Chan.* (The full text is in [`CREDITS.md`](https://github.com/ronalc90/Lenia/blob/main/CREDITS.md).)

Chan's WebGL demos were read as a reference and **reimplemented from scratch**: there is no code from his shaders in Bioluma.

## Scientific papers

- Bert Wang-Chak Chan. **"Lenia: Biology of Artificial Life."** *Complex Systems* 28(3), 2019. [arXiv:1812.05433](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.05433)
- Bert Wang-Chak Chan. **"Lenia and Expanded Universe."** *ALIFE 2020*. [arXiv:2005.03742](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.03742)

## How the game decides what each creature does

The detector's ideas (dead, exploded, stable, swims, spins, pulses, divides) are inspired by published research, and were reimplemented without copying code:

- **Flowers team (Inria)**: [sensorimotor-lenia-search](https://github.com/flowersteam/sensorimotor-lenia-search)
- **Leniabreeder**, by Maxence Faldor and Antoine Cully (ALIFE 2024): [github.com/maxencefaldor/Leniabreeder](https://github.com/maxencefaldor/Leniabreeder)
- The Calibrator ranges follow the sweep published by Hudcova and colleagues ([arXiv:2601.01932](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01932)).

## Sound

**All sound is made on the spot** with Web Audio: oscillators, noise, filters and envelopes. There are no recordings and no audio files.

## Art

**There is no AI-generated art.** The app icon and the game icons are drawn as SVG. The dish, the creatures and the colours are drawn live by the simulation. The Bestiary portraits and the screenshots in this wiki come from the game itself.
