# The Tree 🌳

The **Tree** is where you spend your **Data**. Every upgrade is **yours forever**: it is never lost.

<p align="center"><img src="images/en-09-tree.png" alt="The Tree: the night in the centre and eight paths" width="300"></p>

## How to use it

1. When a session ends, tap **"Go to the Tree"**. Between sessions there is also the **Tree** button at the bottom.
2. Upgrades that **glow green** can be bought now.
3. **Tap one.** Its sheet says what it does with two boxes: **Now** and **With one more level**.
4. Tap **"Buy"**. Done! You will notice it next session.

<p align="center"><img src="images/en-10-node-sheet.png" alt="An upgrade's sheet" width="260"></p>

- **Why does it cost that?** Tap **"Why does it cost this?"** on the sheet: you will see the maths.
- Each level costs a bit more than the one before.
- The **"?"** are upgrades you do not know yet: they appear when you buy the one before.
- Some upgrades wait for a new **night**: their sheet says so. See [[Night|Night-en]].

## The eight paths

**Eight straight paths** leave the centre: 62 upgrades in all. Each step only needs the one before it.

| Path | What it improves | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| ⏱ **Clock** | More time in every session and a faster start | More time, Big clock, Fridge, Final stretch, Hurry requests |
| 💧 **Dropper** | More seeds live and you sow more | Dropper, Pocket glow, Gift seeds, Strong seeds, Auto-seeder |
| 🧫 **Dish** | More room for creatures | Bigger dish, More room, Nursery, Incubator |
| 🌱 **Life** | Every creature gives more Glow | Culture, Sharp edges, Superfood, Swimmers, Friendship, Eternal life |
| ↓ **Collect** | Pipettes that collect the Glow for you | Pipette, Fast pipettes, Big drops, More pipettes, Magnet pipettes |
| 🔬 **Discover** | More Data for each discovery | Field notebook, Copier, Collector, Archive, Microscope |
| 🌍 **Worlds** | New rules with new creatures | World 2 · Cold, World 3 · Whirls… See [[Worlds|Worlds-en]] |
| ✦ **Pulse** | Tapping gives more Glow, and the golden spark gives more | Pulse, Long streak, Flash after flash, Frequent spark, Better gifts |

## Tips

- **Start with what you notice most:** the **Dropper** (more seeds live), **More time** (longer sessions) and the **Pipette** (collects Glow for you).
- **Bigger dish** makes room for more creatures: more Glow at once.
- Like tapping? The **✦ Pulse** route makes every tap and every streak give more.
- **A new world** brings new species, and every new species gives **+10 Data** and **+3 seconds** of clock.
- Short of Data? The sheet tells you **how many** and **in about how many sessions** you will have them.
