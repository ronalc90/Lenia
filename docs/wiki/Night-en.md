# The night 🌙

At Vigil Station the night lasts for months. In Bioluma, **the night moves on** when your lab has grown enough. It is the game's "prestige", but **nothing is erased**.

## When it can move on

The **centre of the Tree** shows two bars: **sessions** and **species**. When both are full, the centre **glows gold**.

| To reach | Sessions | Species |
|---|---|---|
| Night 2 | 3 | 2 |
| Night 3 | 6 | 4 |
| Night 4 | 10 | 7 |
| Night 5 | 14 | 10 |

And so on up to Night 9.

## What happens when it moves on

1. Tap the centre of the Tree and **"New night"**. It is free.
2. VELA lights the lamp (the first time she asks you something 😉).
3. **A new ring of the Tree opens**: upgrades that were in the fog before.
4. Every night gives you **+10% Data** at the end of each session.

**What stays with you:** your Data, the whole Tree, the Bestiary, the worlds you opened, achievements and the story.

**What does not change:** the next session starts as always, with a clean dish and your starting Essence.

## What about Extinction and Genome?

They were the prestige of an earlier version of Bioluma. They are gone: the **night** replaces them and never takes anything away. If you had an old save, the game turned it into **Data and Tree upgrades** ("Welcome to the new lab!").
